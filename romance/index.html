How Romance Works

Build relationships by talking daily, giving gifts, and completing favors. As friendship hearts increase, special cutscenes trigger:

  1. 2 Hearts — First unique dialogue, character opens up about their background.
  2. 4 Hearts — A small favor quest that deepens your bond.
  3. 6 Hearts — A significant personal story event (varies by character).
    4. li>8 Hearts — Romantic confession scene. You can choose to pursue romance or stay friends.
  4. 10 Hearts — Proposal becomes available. Give a Bouquet to officially date, then a Mermaid's Pendant to propose.
💡 Pro Tip: You can romance multiple characters simultaneously until you give the Bouquet. After that, you're committed! Choose wisely.

Hidden Candidates

Two secret romanceable characters require special conditions:

  • Caldarus (the Dragon) — Complete the main storyline, reach max Town Renown, and find him in the Ancient Ruins. Requires Dragon Language skill perk unlocked.
  • Seridia (the Witch) — Gift her 10 Strange Dolls found throughout the world, then visit her hut during a full moon in Fall Year 2+.

Marriage & Children

After proposing and holding the wedding (3-day countdown), your spouse moves onto your farm. Benefits include:

  • Daily gift from spouse — they cook or forage one item per morning
  • Spouse can help with farm chores — watering, feeding animals (configurable)
  • Children option — after 7 days of marriage, ask about having kids; baby arrives after 14 more days
  • Max 2 children per save file
