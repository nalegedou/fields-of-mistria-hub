🎁 Gift Guide

Loved/Liked/Hated gifts for every NPC in Mistria. Daily bonus rules, birthday multipliers, and safe universal gift recommendations.

Gift Mechanics

  • Daily limit: One gift per NPC per day (gives +20 Loved / +10 Liked / +3 Neutral).
  • Birthday bonus: Gifts on an NPC's birthday give 8× normal friendship points. Always save your best gifts for birthdays!
  • Weekly limit: You can only gain friendship from the first 2 gifts per NPC per week (Mon–Sun). Extra gifts still show dialogue but give 0 points.
  • Quality matters: Gold-star items give +50% more points than base quality.
💡 Universal Safe Gifts: If you're not sure what to give, Sushi Platter and Pumpkin Pie are liked or loved by nearly everyone and are easy to cook once you have the recipes.
