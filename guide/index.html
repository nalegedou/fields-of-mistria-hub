📖 Complete Beginner Guide

New to Fields of Mistria? This guide covers everything from your first morning to late-game optimization. Follow along step by step.

What is Fields of Mistria?

Fields of Mistria is a cozy farming RPG developed by No Sun Studios and published by Haunted Hollow Games. You arrive in the charming town of Mistria as a new farmer tasked with helping rebuild after a series of magical earthquakes damaged the town's infrastructure.

The game blends classic farming sim mechanics with RPG elements including combat in mines, a deep romance system with 12 marriage candidates, skill trees across 9 disciplines, cooking, fishing, foraging, and a central "Town Repair" storyline that unlocks new areas and features as you progress.

🎮 Platform: Available on PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, Xbox. This guide covers all platforms — controls may vary slightly.

Your First Week: Day-by-Day

Day 1 - Arrival

  • You'll be introduced to the town and given your starting farm plot.
  • Clean up your farm — clear rocks, weeds, logs, and till 15+ plots.
  • Buy Parsnip seeds from the general store (10g each). Plant them all — they grow in 3 days.
  • Meet everyone — talk to each NPC at least once to start building relationships.
  • Forage — pick up Dandelions, Wild Horseradish, and anything else on the ground. Free money!

Day 2-3 - Establishing Routine

  • Water your crops every morning — they don't grow if you skip even one day.
  • Fish at least once per day — even basic fish sell for decent early-game cash.
  • Mine when it rains — you don't need to water crops on rainy days! Use that time for mining floors.
  • Save for a Backpack upgrade — the default 15-slot inventory fills up fast.

Day 4-7 - First Harvest & Expansion

  • Harvest your Parsnips! Sell them or save some for energy food.
  • Plant higher-value crops — Potatoes (6 days, 140g sell) are great for Spring Year 1.
  • Repair your first bridge — this unlocks new foraging areas and is part of the Town Repair questline.
  • Consider buying a second set of tools — copper tools become available as you progress.

Money-Making Tips

  1. Fish on rainy days — Rain increases fish spawn rates and quality. Some fish only appear in rain.
  2. Plant regrowable crops — Strawberries, Tomatoes, Blueberries, Grapes, and Cranberries pay for themselves many times over.
  3. Cook before selling — Most cooked dishes sell for more than their raw ingredient value. Even simple dishes like Fried Egg (+30g over raw egg) add up.
  4. Mine for gems — Gems (Amethyst, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond) have consistently high sell prices regardless of season.
  5. Don't ignore foraging — Free items like Dandelions, Wild Horseradish, and mushrooms provide reliable daily income with zero investment.
  6. Level up skills quickly — Higher skill levels unlock perks that increase crop quality, reduce energy cost, and boost sell prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I marry any character?

There are 12 marriageable characters in Mistria (as of v1.0). Each has a unique heart event sequence leading to proposal. Two hidden candidates (Caldarus the dragon and Seridia the witch) require special conditions to unlock. See our Romance Guide for details.

What happens if I pass out vs. die in the mines?

Passing out (from exhaustion at 2AM) costs 10% of your gold, max 1,000g. Dying in the mines costs more (up to 25% of gold) and you lose some items. You always wake up in your bed or the clinic the next morning.

Is there multiplayer?

No — Fields of Mistria is single-player only. There are no current plans for multiplayer support.

How do I unlock the Greenhouse?

The Greenhouse becomes available as a Town Repair project after you reach the bottom of the mines (Floor 120) and donate the required materials (Gold Bars and Iridium). It allows year-round farming of any season's crops.
