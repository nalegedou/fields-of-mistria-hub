100+ recipes with full ingredient lists, cooking levels, energy recovery values, and every method to unlock new dishes.

How Cooking Works

Cooking in Fields of Mistria is done at the kitchen in your farmhouse or at the kitchen station in town. You need:

  • The correct ingredients in your inventory
  • A sufficient Cooking Skill Level (some recipes require Lv3+, Lv5+, etc.)
  • To have unlocked the recipe> — see below for unlock methods

How to Unlock Recipes
