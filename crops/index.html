🌱 Crops List & Farming Guide

All 60+ crops in Fields of Mistria — seasons, seed costs, sell prices, growth times, and which crops give you the most gold per day.

Farming Overview

Farming is the backbone of your income in Fields of Mistria. Each season brings different crops with varying growth cycles, seed costs, and sell values. The key to maximizing profit is understanding profit per day, not just total sell price.

💡 Pro Tip: Always check if a crop is regrowable (produces multiple harvests from one planting). Regrowable crops like Strawberries and Tomatoes often outperform single-harvest crops over a full season, even if their per-harvest value seems lower.

Crop Quality & Fertilizer Tips

  • Basic Quality Fertilizer — Increases chance of Silver-quality harvest. Apply every day for best results.
  • Quality Fertilizer — Increases chance of Gold-quality harvest. Expensive but worth it on high-value crops like Starfruit and Pumpkin.
  • Speed-Grow Fertilizer — Reduces grow time by 1 day (minimum 1 day). Best used on slow crops (Cauliflower, Melon, Starfruit).
  • Watering Can Upgrades — Higher-level cans water more tiles at once. Prioritize this upgrade before Year 2 summer.
