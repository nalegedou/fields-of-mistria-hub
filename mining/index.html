⛏️ Mining & Combat Guide

All 5 mine biomes, ore/gem drops, monster strategies, and how to unlock every Seal for deeper exploration.

The Five Mine Biomes

The mines of Mistria are divided into 5 distinct biomes, each with unique resources, enemies, and environmental hazards:

Combat Tips

  • Upgrade your weapon early — Copper Sword → Steel Sword → Gold Sword. Each tier significantly increases damage.
  • Use bombs strategically — Bombs damage all enemies in a radius and break rocks simultaneously. Essential for crowded rooms.
  • Eat food before descending — Cooked dishes provide temporary combat buffs (attack +10%, defense +15%, etc.).
  • Learn enemy patterns — Each enemy type has tells before attacking. Slimes hop, bats dive, golems wind up.
  • Don't greed for resources at low HP — Dying in the mines costs up to 25% of your gold. Know when to retreat.
💡 Best Practice: Always bring at least 30 food-restored energy worth of items when going below Floor 40. The Deep Earth biome has long stretches between food drops.
