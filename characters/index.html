Main Storyline Walkthrough

Phase 1: Arrival & First Repairs (Days 1–14)

  1. Arrive in Mistria, meet Mayor Thalia and receive your farm.
  2. Clear your farm plot and plant first crops.
  3. Meet all townsfolk — each introduction adds to your Town Renown.
  4. Repair the Main Bridge (500 wood + 300 stone) — unlocks western farmland.

Phase 2: Growing the Community (Weeks 2–6)

  1. Donate to the Community Center bundles — unlocks new areas and recipes.
  2. Reach Floor 20 of the mines — unlocks Copper tools.
  3. Repair the Mine Elevator — allows fast travel between mine sections.
  4. Befriend key NPCs to trigger their first Heart Events.

Phase 3: Mid-Game Expansion (Months 2–4)

  1. Unlock the Greenhouse via Town Repair — year-round farming!
  2. Reach Floor 100 of mines — access Ancient Ruins and legendary loot.
  3. Progress romance to 10 hearts — propose to your chosen partner.
  4. Unlock all Seals for full mine exploration.

Phase 4: Endgame (Year 2+)

  1. Complete all Town Repair projects — fully restored Mistria.
  2. Marry, have children, max out all skills.
  3. Discover hidden candidates (Caldarus, Seridia).
  4. Achieve Perfection — 100% completion including all achievements.

Town Renown System

Town Renown measures how much the community trusts you. Higher ranks unlock:

  • Rank 1 (Newcomer): Basic shop access
  • Rank 2 (Helper): New seeds available at General Store
  • Rank 3 (Trusted): Discount at all shops (10%)
  • Rank 4 (Respected): Unlock special quests from Mayor Thalia
  • Rank 5 (Hero): Access to final story content + secret ending
💡 Fastest Way to Raise Renown: Complete Town Repair projects (+50 each), participate in festivals (+10–30), give gifts to non-romanceable villagers (+2/day), and finish NPC favor requests (+15 each).
