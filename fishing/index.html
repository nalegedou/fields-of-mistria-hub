Fishing Mechanics

  • The Bar Mini-game: Cast your line, wait for a bite, then hold to keep the fish inside the moving bar. Larger/rarer fish have smaller bars and faster movement.
  • Fishing Level Matters: Higher levels increase bar size, slow down fish escape speed, and unlock new tackle options.
  • Bait & Tackle: Use bait to increase bite rate. Different tackle types affect bar behavior (e.g., Trap Bobber pauses the bar).
  • Weather Effects: Rain increases bite rates significantly. Some fish only appear during rain or specific weather.
💡 Pro Tip: The Dressed Spinner tackle is the best all-rounder — it slightly increases bar size AND slows fish movement. Craft it as soon as you unlock the recipe.

Legendary Fish

There are 5 Legendary Fish in Fields of Mistria. Each can only be caught once per save file and has very specific requirements:

  1. Legend of the Lake — Mountain Lake, any season, clear weather only, Fishing Lv5+, sell 2,000g
  2. Abyssal Serpent — Mines Floor 100+, any time, must have Iron Rod or better, sell 3,000g
  3. Crimson King Crab — Beach, Summer only, rainy evening (6PM–2AM), sell 2,500g
  4. Glacial Sturgeon — Town River, Winter only, requires Ice Fishing upgrade, sell 2,800g
  5. Golden Koi — Secret pond (unlocked via Town Repair #5), Spring, sunny morning, sell 5,000g
