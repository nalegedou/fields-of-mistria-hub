⭐ Skills & Perks

All 9 skill trees fully mapped: tier-by-tier perks, Essence costs, unlock levels, and our recommended priority order for efficient progression.

How Skills Work

Each action you take in Mistria earns XP toward the relevant skill. When a skill levels up (max Level 10), you earn Essence Points that can be spent on that skill's perk tree. There are 5 tiers of perks per skill:

TierUnlock LevelEssence CostPerk Power
Tier 1Lv 11 pointSmall quality-of-life boost
Tier 2Lv 52 pointsModerate gameplay improvement
Tier 3Lv 154 pointsSignificant power increase
Tier 4Lv 308 pointsMajor game-changing ability
Tier 5Lv 45–6012+ pointsUltimate / capstone perk
